# Installing the Edge CRM app

For sales executives, and for whoever sets up their phones. You need your CRM email and password.

## What the app does

Your day in one place: follow-ups that are due, new leads and unassigned leads to claim, with one-tap Call and WhatsApp. After a
call or message, it asks how it went and helps you set the next follow-up.

- **Leads:** everything about a lead, and edit it. The **⋮** button at the top right has the less common actions.
- **Tasks:** your follow-ups and tasks by due date. Tap the circle to finish one, or open it to comment or change it.
- **Me:** your month, then **Work** for Projects, Deposits and the Leaderboard. Managers also get the Dashboard, deposit review
  and project settings.

You see the same things you can do on the website. Notes and finished tasks save with no signal and send when you're back online.
Other changes need a connection.

## Install

1. Open the install link on the phone.
2. If you see **Download anyway**, tap it, then tap **Open**.
3. If the phone says it can't install unknown apps, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, and go back.
4. Tap **Install**.
5. Google Play Protect may say **App scan recommended**, with only **Scan app** and **Don't install app** to choose from. Tap
   **Scan app**. This is expected: Google hasn't seen this private company app before. When the scan finishes, tap **Install**.

Samsung phones may refuse before any of this. Go to **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker**, turn it off,
install the app, then turn it back on.

## First sign-in

1. Sign in with your CRM email and password. A temporary password has to be changed first. Tap the eye to check what you typed.
2. Tap **Get started**, and allow **Notifications** so you hear about new leads and follow-ups.
3. The first time you tap **Call**, allow **Phone**. If you don't, the phone's dialer opens with the number filled in instead.

Accounts with admin access also need a code from an authenticator app. If the app says to set one up, tap **Open CRM**, turn it
on there, then come back and sign in.

## Call tracking

An optional extra that saves you typing. It's on unless you turn off **Log call length automatically** when you first sign in.
Change it any time under **Me > Settings > Call tracking**.

- It saves the number, time, direction and length of your calls with leads, on this phone.
- Calls to anyone who isn't a lead are not saved.
- WhatsApp and Telegram calls aren't included. Add those by hand after the call.
- Nothing is ever recorded: the app can't hear your calls.
- With it off, the app works as normal. Your manager can see that it's off.

If Android says a permission is a **restricted setting**, open **Settings → Apps → Edge CRM**, tap the **⋮** menu at the top
right, choose **Allow restricted settings**, and try again.

### Keep it running on your brand of phone

Many phones close apps to save battery, which can pause tracking. Do this once:

| Phone                  | Setting                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Samsung                | Settings → Apps → Edge CRM → Battery → **Unrestricted**. Also check it isn't under Battery → Background usage limits → Sleeping apps |
| Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco    | Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Edge CRM → **Autostart on**, and Battery saver → **No restrictions**                                 |
| Oppo, Realme           | Settings → Apps → Edge CRM → Battery → **Allow background activity** and **Allow auto launch**                                       |
| Vivo, iQOO             | Settings → Battery → Background power consumption → Edge CRM → **Allow**                                                             |
| OnePlus                | Settings → Apps → Edge CRM → Battery → **Unrestricted**                                                                              |
| Pixel, Motorola, Nokia | Settings → Apps → Edge CRM → App battery usage → **Unrestricted**                                                                    |

Menu names vary a little between versions.

Don't use **Force stop** on the app. It pauses tracking until you next open it. Your calls are still picked up then.

### Checking it works

Open **Me > Settings > Call tracking**. It should say **On**. Make a short call to a lead and check that it appears on the lead's
timeline within a minute or so.

If something looks wrong, open **Technical details**, tap **Share report** and send it to your manager. The report doesn't contain
phone numbers.

## Updates

When a new version is ready, the app asks when you open it. Tap **Update**. The first time, Android asks to allow Edge CRM to
install apps: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** and go back. Then close the app, open it and tap **Update**
again. After that, updates take one tap and you stay signed in.
